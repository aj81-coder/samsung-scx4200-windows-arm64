# Development transparency

This project began as a personal attempt to keep a Samsung SCX-4200 working on a Windows 11 ARM64 laptop. It is shared so others can benefit, inspect it and improve it.

## AI's role

OpenAI's ChatGPT and Codex were used extensively throughout development (“vibe coding”). AI proposed and selected much of the architecture, made substantial technical decisions, implemented and debugged the original project code, planned and wrote automated checks, interpreted diagnostic reports, and generated the documentation and release notes. This disclosure covers the project documentation as well as its implementation.

## Human involvement

The owner defined the practical goal and constraints, supplied feedback and diagnostic reports, chose project policies such as licensing and publication, and performed the physical printer tests requested during development. Another household user has also printed a document normally without the owner's help. Human observations establish actual paper output; an AI-generated test report alone cannot establish that.

## What the evidence means

Basic printing functions have been tested and the app is now in regular household use on the reported Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge. Automated checks, physical tests and their remaining limits are described in VALIDATION.md. These are not an independent human expert audit of the source, a certification, or proof of compatibility with every printer, laptop, application and document.

Existing upstream libraries and the SpliX-derived renderer retain their authorship credits and component licences. AI involvement does not replace those projects' contributions or relicense their work. Original project code is Apache-2.0; read LICENSING.md for the complete boundaries.

Contributions and reproducible reports are welcome. Do not include private print documents or unreviewed diagnostic archives in public reports.
