Next target check: cancel a waiting job

Use the SAME0.7.0 folder which passed ordinary printing and paper refill.
Keep the existing SCX-4200 Capture Test printer entry. No new download needed.
This intentionally cancels a sample job; a stopped/error result is expected.

1. Close any previous bridge window. Switch the printer on and connect USB.
   Remove unused paper from its INPUT tray. Keep the tray empty throughout
   this cancellation check, even after you cancel the document.
2. Run 1-START-RELIABLE-BRIDGE.cmd. Type ARM, press Enter and wait for readiness.
3. Open TWO-PAGE-SAMPLE.pdf and print ALLpages, ONEcopy, A4, one-sided to
   SCX-4200 Capture Test. Submit it once. Wait about20seconds for the printer
   to need paper / bridge to report waiting. Do NOT refill the tray this time.
4. Open Windows Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners >
   SCX-4200 Capture Test > Open print queue. Select the sample document and
   use Cancel (right-click or its three-dot menu). Cancel only that test job.
   If the queue has no document, or the bridge already says Job completed,
   do not submit again. Collect results and report that observation instead;
   this run did not catch the job while it was still waiting.
5. Watch the bridge for a cancelled/stopped/failed message. If it is still
   waiting after about15seconds, record that fact; do not submit another job.
   Press Ctrl+C and allow up to a minute for the bridge program to stop.
6. Run 2-COLLECT-RESULTS.cmd and attach the reliable-bridge-results ZIP here.
   Tell us whether Cancel stopped the waiting job, the message shown, and
   whether the document disappeared from the Windows queue. Keep the tray
   empty and printer off once the bridge is stopped, until recovery is reviewed.

Cancellation stops further sending. Data already accepted by the printer
cannot be recalled, so do not refill and accidentally print buffered output.
Don't press Print again, restart the bridge, delete a lock, or run the recovery
tool during this first check. The recovery step will follow review of its logs.

If the startup check is blocked, or the test document remains in the queue,
collect the results and report that instead. Do not remove the printer entry
or change other printers/spooler services. Interrupted/partial delivery is
expected to remain blocked until a deliberate reset abandons old printer data.
